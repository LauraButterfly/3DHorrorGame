# Cute but Scary


### Isabella King, Laura Rissler, Melissa Tan, Matt Gallenberger



### Description of current target for the game

Cute but Scary is a horror game where you solve puzzles to save your friend who is trapped in a haunted house. You roam the house and have to find three keys that will allow you to unlock the cage that your friend is trapped in. As you explore the house, you need to avoid the monster that creeps around the house. When it comes near you can hide under tables, chair, and any small space. The monster gets increasingly smarter as you obtain keys by becoming slightly faster and more likely to move in your direction. The player can hide, remain idle, move, interact with objects, and sprint. If you do get caught, your character will get placed in the cage and you will respawn as a different Sanrio character who is tasked with saving two friends.
